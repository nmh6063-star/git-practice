# How Can AI Make Good Software Developers Better
The article that will be discussed is: https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbestechcouncil/2026/09/21/how-can-ai-make-good-software-developers-better/

## What is Discussed / What do I Think?
The article argues that "Vibe Coding" can arguably improve the output of software developers. More specifically, "Vibe Coding" is only a powerful tool if the user has a proper understanding of the programming language and a proper understanding of the system they are trying to create with AI tools. The article states that by having the correct technical knowledge, any faults made by the AI can be accounted for and corrected, which can improve productivity without reducing quality. Additionally, it can serve as a critic of the user's work to check for edge cases potentially never thought of. I find it interesting that it defends the whole persona of "Vibe Coding". I personally have a bit of a distaste towards the whole idea of "Vibe Coding" as it takes away the part of directly writing code which contributes to me feeling like I am actually doing something. Additionally, I feel as though I lose critical thinking skills when it comes to programming every time I ask an AI model to review my work instead of reviewing it myself, as I become dependent on an AI telling me what should be and shouldn't be correct. Additionally, if the AI gets it wrong, I won't know immediately, and I will spend time questioning my own understanding and waste time trying to figure out whether I'm right or wrong.
